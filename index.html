I build AI systems that go beyond demos: agents that choose their own tools, and retrieval you can trust. Grounded in data, guarded by design, tested before they ship.

I'm an Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science graduate (Arab Open University, 2026) focused on LLM applications, RAG systems and tool-calling agents. I work with Python, FastAPI and LangChain, and I back them with solid backend skills in Laravel, REST APIs and MySQL.

My projects include an enterprise RAG assistant with hybrid search, source attribution and department-level access control, and a customer support agent that picks its own tools, retries on failure and hands off to a human when the rules say so.

Beyond code, I trained in Generative AI engineering (agents, MCP, guardrails, LLM evaluation) and Business Intelligence at ITI, and I served as Head of HR at Google Developer Student Clubs, coordinating recruitment, workshops and events.
